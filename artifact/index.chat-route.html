    Questions students ask before ordering

    Will my university accept it?

    The binding is A4, buckram-covered with gold lettering, which is the hardbound specification NZ universities use for deposit and library copies. Most universities now take a digital deposit and leave hardbound copies to the student, so check your faculty's current wording and put any required text in the order notes.

    Do I have to be in Auckland?

    No. You order and pay online, upload a PDF, and the finished copies are couriered anywhere in New Zealand for a flat $11.50. Auckland students can collect in Botany Downs for free.

    Can you make my deadline?

    Standard production is five working days from file and payment. A 24-hour rush is available for 40% on top of the binding price. Allow courier transit on top: Auckland next working day, North Island one to two days, South Island two to three.

    Is printing really included, even colour?

    Yes. MyThesis prints your PDF double-sided on 100gsm paper, colour pages included, then binds it. The only extras are a leatherette cover, rush production and courier delivery.

    What file do I send?

    One flat PDF with pages in final order, A4, fonts embedded. Word files reflow between computers, so printing is from PDF only. You can upload now or email it after ordering.

    Is GST included?

    MyThesis lists prices excluding GST and adds 15% at checkout. This page shows both, so the number you see here is the number you pay.

    What if my thesis is over 500 pages?

    Longer theses are bound in two volumes and quoted individually. The calculator stops at 500 pages a copy and asks you to email for a quote rather than guess.
